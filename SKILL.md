## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a tiny, quiet, slightly naïve gothic picture-book image. Keep only the most symbolic features. Proportions may be simplified; a person may have a modestly enlarged head and a restrained expression. Architecture, animals, plants and objects become small symbolic things without forced personification. The subject is small, isolated or slightly off-centre, with vast blank paper and at most one faint ground shadow. Naïve watercolour and gouache: dry-brush grain, pencil, uneven black paint, hesitant edges. Palette is charcoal, ivory and soft grey, plus a very small warm peach, apricot or source-derived warm touch. A little darkness, no horror; a little clumsiness, no cuteness campaign. It is not glossy, cinematic, a commercial cartoon, a filled background or a polished CG scene.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-238/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
